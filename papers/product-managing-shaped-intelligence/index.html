Paper purpose

  • It evaluates one bounded Product-managing shaped-intelligence evidence object and one completed programme instance within that evidence boundary, established through the direct-shaping approach.
  • It examines recurring product-coordination burden across the AI Shaping knowledge and publication product.

Abstract

Product-managing shaped intelligence is evaluated within the bounded AI Shaping knowledge and publication product. Established through the direct-shaping approach, the capability is tested against three separate propositions: bounded operational maturity; observable but unquantified coordination value and work-burden shift; and a limited first-party contribution argument that direct shaping can create operational value in this context.

The evidence is a purposive eight-case first-party corpus and a dated seventeen-cycle evidence window, supplemented by the completed 30 July 2026 cross-surface harmonisation programme as one bounded retrospective instance. Maturity was withheld after two complete cycles and the predeclared three-cycle gate was satisfied by the third; later accepted cycles show persistence and variation, not effect size.

The selected evidence supports the three bounded propositions within this product while human product ownership, stewardship and decision authority remain controlling. It remains first-party, retrospective, purposively sampled, unequal in trace depth, unquantified and not independently validated or replicated. Section 10 contains the controlling test and Section 11 the complete limitations.

Public contribution

  • Classifies Product-managing shaped intelligence as operationally mature within the bounded product.
  • Evaluates observable but unquantified Product-coordination value and work-burden shift.
  • Contributes bounded first-party evidence that direct shaping can create operational value in this context.
  • Uses a purposive eight-case corpus, a dated seventeen-cycle evidence window and an explicit three-cycle maturity gate.
  • Centralises first-party, retrospective, measurement, circularity, independent-validation, replication and transfer limitations.

Public boundary

The paper supports the bounded operational-maturity classification, observable but unquantified coordination value and a limited first-party direct-approach contribution argument only. It does not establish quantified effect, sole causation, independent validation, replication, generic transferability, autonomy, universal Product-management capability, complete Scrum practice, implementation readiness, deployment assurance or general superiority of the direct-shaping approach.
