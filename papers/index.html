Published three-paper set

AI shaping papers: category definition and two capability evidence papers

The Category Definition paper owns the shared concept architecture. The Project-managing and Product-managing Capability Evidence papers evaluate distinct shaped-intelligence instances established through the mediated-shaping and direct-shaping approaches.

Formal public v1.86 · Three DOI-backed working papers · Separate evidence objects and limitations.

Choose a paper

Start with the lightest useful route

Use the Category Definition paper for category fit and concept architecture. Use the Project-managing evidence paper for the mediated project-work instance. Use the Product-managing evidence paper for the direct-shaping bounded-product instance. Each paper remains independently readable and controls a different public question.

Latest version and DOI route

Current published paper set: v1.86 · paper files last updated 27 July 2026, 3:21 pm AEST

All three papers are DOI-backed public working papers published on Zenodo. Use the landing pages and DOI records for version access, citation details and the public-use boundary.

The wider product relationship is explained on Product-managing shaped intelligence; publication does not remove the separate evidence objects or expand their permitted conclusions.

Use boundary

The papers support category understanding and bounded public-stage evidence evaluation. They do not provide implementation guidance, deployment assurance, reproducibility proof, measured-improvement proof, method transfer or commercial-use authority.

How the three papers fit together

The Category Definition paper owns the shared architecture. The Project-managing paper evaluates the distinct mediated-shaping project-work instance. The Product-managing paper evaluates the distinct direct-shaping bounded-product instance. Publication of the Product-managing paper does not establish quantified burden reduction, measured productivity improvement, independent validation, autonomy, generic transferability, universal Product-management capability, complete Scrum practice, deployment assurance or general superiority of direct shaping.
